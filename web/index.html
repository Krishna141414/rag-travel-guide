    Limitations

    • Small LLM. Qwen2.5-0.5B gives short, sometimes minimal answers ("December 1961") and writes its own [n] citation only 1 time in 7; the rest are matched by word overlap and shown dashed as [n]*.
    • Thin threshold margin. Out-of-scope questions scored 0.32-0.49 and answerable ones 0.62-0.77 in the evaluation, but a paraphrase like "houseboat cruise through backwaters" scores only 0.41 and gets refused, while "capital of Brazil" reaches 0.47 by matching Goa's Portuguese history.
    • CPU speed. Answers take ~2-5 s after the models load; the first request after starting the app takes longer.
    • Small library. Only 25 Indian destinations from a Wikipedia snapshot: anything outside them, or newer than the snapshot, is "not in documents".
    • No reranker or hybrid search. A name-only question like "What is the Hawa Mahal?" retrieves Hampi first; BM25 + vectors or a cross-encoder would help (Task 9).
